# WhiteBucket Explains: video numbers

The **Analyst** reads this list and tells the Chief of Staff what's working. Once a week
(Saturday morning is best), check each posted video's numbers in the app and add one line
per video per platform. Or just tell Claude the numbers and it adds the lines.

    - YYYY-MM-DD | Platform | Video title | Series | views | likes | new follows

- **Date** = the day you checked (not the day it was posted).
- **Series**: What If, How It Works, World News, AI Village, Island Clip, or Other.
- Likes and new follows are optional: leave them out or write `-`.
- Adding a newer line for the same video and platform replaces the older one (no need to delete).

Example: `- 2026-11-21 | TikTok | What If You Fell Into a Black Hole? | What If | 1250 | 84 | 3`

Only numbers you can see in the apps. No passwords, logins or account details here.

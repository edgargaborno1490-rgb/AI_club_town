# Analyst notes for the Chief of Staff

What the numbers say beyond views: watch time, who watches, what made people follow.
Newest first. The Chief reads this with `village/analyst-report.md` before picking a topic.
These are recommendations; rotation and format changes stay the owner's call.

## 2026-11-17 (from the owner's TikTok and YouTube screenshots)

**Working**
- **The island itself.** "Meet WhiteBucket and His AI Village" (YouTube Short, Nov 14):
  1,170 views (+225 in the last 48 hours, still growing), 13 likes, 5 comments,
  **+20 subscribers**. YouTube calls it the channel's most-viewed Short. Those 20 subscribers
  are most of the 25 YouTube followers: the island is what makes people follow.
- **Young audience.** That video's viewers: 56% are 13–17, 25% are 25–34, 0% 18–24.
  Keep hooks simple, fast and visual; keep the AI village / game feel.

**Not working (yet)**
- **Leonids on TikTok:** 144 views but people left fast: average watch **7.2 s of 84 s**,
  only **1.27%** watched it all, 4 likes, 0 comments, 0 new followers.
  Its first ~18 s was an island-update intro (the race chart: "THE RACE … 44 followers"),
  then the meteors. Most viewers were gone before the meteor part started.
- **Leonids on YouTube:** 25 views in 23 hours, 1 like, 3 comments, +1 subscriber.
  The same topic did about 6x better on TikTok than YouTube.

**What the Chief should do with this**
1. The first 3 seconds must be the hook (the strongest picture or question), on every platform.
   The island checkup still comes first (owner's rule, Nov 17), so make the checkup itself the
   hook: show the island moving (birds, the new build) right away, not a chart or a title card,
   and keep it short (aim for 5 s or less before the topic starts).
2. Lean into the island: viewer builds (Birdhouses, Bird Bath), named birds and the follower
   race are the story people subscribed for. Island Clips and the weekly update are good bets.
3. Too early to judge series: only 2 videos have numbers. Need 3 per series.
4. Ask the owner for: the Nov 15 plane Short's numbers, the intro video on TikTok/Instagram/
   Facebook, and the Leonids average watch time on YouTube, so the next report can compare.

**Owner's call (not the Analyst's):** whether the island checkup should be shorter than the
Leonids one, or appear as quick shots instead of a block at the very start.

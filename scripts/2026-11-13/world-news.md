## Narration
Nvidia just lost the CEO who built it, and a spacecraft is about to cross one light-day. Here's your world news, as of November 13. First, Jensen Huang has stepped down as CEO of Nvidia, the chip company behind much of today's AI, after 33 years in charge. The company hasn't given a reason, but reports about his personal life had led to a board investigation. Chief financial officer Colette Kress is now interim CEO, and the stock fell more than 4 percent. Next, Europe is moving closer to the table in the US-brokered talks between Ukraine and Russia. European representatives are set to join the next round, but Moscow hasn't clearly agreed, and big gaps over territory remain. And finally, on November 18, NASA's Voyager 1 will be one light-day from Earth. That's about 26 billion kilometers, so far that a message takes a full day to reach it, and 49 years after launch, it's still talking to us. Follow White Bucket for more.

## Narration (record as one paragraph)

Hi, I'm WhiteBucket… and I don't run this channel alone. I have a whole village. This is AI Club Town, a tiny 3D island where every building is an AI worker with one job. In the town hall lives my Chief of Staff. Every morning, it picks a brand-new topic. The observatory is the What If studio, for the wild science questions. The gear factory is How It Works, where we break down money and the things you use every day. The news tower is World News, and it checks every fact against two different sources. The workshop belongs to the Programmer, who makes sure every script is ready to build. And the bank? That's the Banker, keeping track of every dollar. When a worker is busy, its building glows, and a little speech bubble shows exactly what it's doing. That's real work, not a loop. Then it's my turn. I read every script, and nothing gets made until I say yes. Each series even has its own voice. The goal is one thousand dollars. Right now, the bank says… zero. Every Sunday, I'll show you what we made, what broke, and exactly how much came in. Follow along… and let's see if my village can make its first dollar.
